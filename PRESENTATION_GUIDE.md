## 🎤 Verbatim Presentation Script

### Part 1: The Problem (Minute 0:00 - 1:00)
> *"Hello judges and educators! Over 70 million deaf people communicate using sign language worldwide, yet for hearing students who want to learn, most apps are either passive video dictionaries or basic quizzes that only tell you 'Wrong!' without explaining **why**.*
>
> *Sign language isn't just flat letters—it is a 3D spatial language built on hand shape, palm orientation, and movement. Today, we are proud to introduce **SIGNQUEST: Learn. Sign. Level Up.**"*

### Part 2: The Dashboard & Learning Loop (Minute 1:00 - 2:00)
*(Navigate to Dashboard)*
> *"Here on Alex's Dashboard, learning feels like a premium educational RPG. You see their Level 4 status, total XP, daily streak, and Today's Quest: 'Master Everyday Greetings'.*
>
> *Notice our Curriculum Mastery Breakdown: Alex has achieved 88% on Hand Shape, but only 53% on Hand Orientation. Let's see how our AI diagnoses this in real time."*

### Part 3: Live Computer Vision & The Landmark Mesh (Minute 2:00 - 3:30)
*(Click into Learn Mode on Sign 'B')*
> *"On the left, you see the anatomical target guide for letter 'B'—flat open palm with thumb across, targeting a camera-facing orientation.*
>
> *In the center is our live camera feed running local MediaPipe hand landmark tracking at 30 frames per second. Notice the 21 joints, the bone connections, and the 3D normal vector protruding directly from the center of the palm."*

### Part 4: The Core AI "Aha!" Moment (Minute 3:30 - 4:30)
*(Simulate or perform inward palm)*
> *"Now watch what happens when a beginner performs the sign with their palm turned inward toward their chest. (Click `[⚠ Inward Palm]`).*
>
> *Look at the right-hand AI breakdown! Instead of a generic 'Wrong', our geometric vector classifier isolates the issue: Hand Shape is rated 96% correct, but Palm Orientation drops to 48% with specific feedback:*
> **'Your palm is rotated inward toward yourself. Turn your palm to face outward toward the camera.'**"

### Part 5: Progression & Reward (Minute 4:30 - 5:15)
*(Switch to `[✓ Perfect Sign]`)*
> *"Now the student adjusts their palm outward. Look at the instant response: Confidence climbs to 94%, the emerald green aura pulses, our harmonic Web Audio chime plays, and +100 XP is awarded!"*

### Part 6: Multi-Modal Gameplay (Minute 5:15 - 6:15)
*(Quick tour: Word Builder or Boss Battle)*
> *"Beyond single letters, SIGNQUEST features 6 distinct game modes:*
> - **Word Builder**: Chaining letters into words like H-E-L-L-O and evaluating sequence transitions.
> - **Sign Detective**: Case files where students decode secret signals to solve forensic mysteries.
> - **Boss Battles**: Conquering 'The Silent Guardian', where the boss dynamically targets the student's weakest signs!"

### Part 7: Teacher Dashboard & Cohort Intelligence (Minute 6:15 - 7:15)
*(Navigate to Teacher Portal)*
> *"For schools and educators, we built a dedicated Teacher Dashboard. Respecting student privacy under FERPA and COPPA, teachers see aggregate cohort trends rather than home surveillance.*
>
> *The AI synthesizes class telemetry: '44% of students exhibit palm orientation inversions on sequential signs B and D.' With one click on **[START CLASS CHALLENGE]**, the teacher synchronizes an orientation calibration drill to all 24 connected students!"*

### Part 8: Accessibility, Ethics & Conclusion (Minute 7:15 - 8:00)
*(Toggle High Contrast / Large Text, open Privacy Charter)*
> *"Finally, accessibility is woven into our core: high contrast mode, readable typography, and 100% sound-free usability.*
>
> *All camera frames process locally in the browser—no video is ever stored or uploaded. Sign language isn't about 'fixing' anyone; it is about building bridges of communication and inclusion.*
>
> *Thank you, and welcome to SIGNQUEST: Learn. Sign. Level Up!"*
